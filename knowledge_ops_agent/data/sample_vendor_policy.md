# Vendor Management Policy (v3.2)

## 4. Approval limits
Contracts up to USD 50,000 may be approved by a department head.
Contracts between USD 50,000 and USD 250,000 require the CFO's approval. 
Contracts above USD 250,000 require Board approval.

## 6. Renewal
ALL vendor contracts must be reviewed 90 days before expiry. Auto-renewal
clauses are prohibited unless an exception is granted by Legal.
