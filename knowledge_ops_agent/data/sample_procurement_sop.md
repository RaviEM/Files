# Procurement SPO-017: Onboarding a new vendor

1. Procurement raises a vendor request in the ERP system.
2. Finance performs a credit and sanctions check (target: 5 business days).
3. Legal reviews the draft contract. Contracts over USD 100,000 additionally require a security questionnaire from the vendor.
4. The approver defined in the Vendor Management Policy signs the contract.
5. Procurement activates the vendor record.
